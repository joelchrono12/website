## Watching

During my days home alone, I watched a couple of pictures that happen to be both directed by Steven Spielberg, so that was fun. Zero complaints!

- 🤖 **A.I. Artificial Intelligence** - This movie follows the journey of a sentient robot, shaped after a child, built to seek the love of a mother whose actual child is in a comma. The robot really does behave like a kid, in both good and bad ways, but when the real child returns, things turn complicated and Daniel, our protagonist, ends up abandoned on the woods. His child-like mind dreams of becoming a real boy. It is a bit of a retelling of Pinnocchio, but in a very different context that was super interesting to me. Spielberg worked alongside Stanley Kubrick to write it, though Kubrick passed before the film was done. Loved it.

- 🏃 **Catch Me If You Can** - This is the movie of a con man who commited check fraud, identity fraud, and all sorts of things, lading a fortune and travelling world wide, pretending to be a pilot, a doctor and even a lawyer. This was apparently based on real life, but it seems like the guy actually made everything up, so in a way he was a pretty good con man anyway. The movie itself is a roller coaster though and extremely entertaining, as Tom Hanks is the FBI agent obsessed with capturing this guy played by Leo Di Captrio. Amazing duo!

## Gaming

### Completed

- 🔬 **The Two Rivers Hypothesis** - *Wait, where did this come from? A completed videogame in less than a week?* Well, don't be too surprised, this is an existential horror game where we control a remote submarine as it ventures into a laboratory installation where some physical theories and anomalies were studied. It is mostly about finding knowledge as you move forward, and understanding (or being confused) by the revelations of what happened there. It's only about thirty minutes long, and [you can get it for free on itch.io!](https://grubwall.itch.io/the-two-rivers-hypothesis)

- 🕵️ **Emio: The Smiling Man** - *Wait, where did this come from? A completed game in less than a week?* Well, you should be surprised! This is a visual novel---not a genre for everyone it seems---where we play as a young detective solving a murder mystery. The story starts with a young student found dead, with a paperbag on his head with a creepy smiling face drawn on it. The plot thickens at every moment, and the resolution is rather awesome. I played this in three days, up to 5 hours each session. I will write a review for it very soon, though I don't want to spoil anything else. For a Nintendo game, this deals with very dark topics and some gruesome moments, so discretion is advised!
