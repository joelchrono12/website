- 💻 Work has been interesting. My coworker asked for some vacations days and I've been covering for them. At this point it's nothing new for me and I'm able to handle things just fine. The company has been going through some rough stuff so I'm only happy to be able to prove myself!

I felt really good about it all though and I'm glad my audience---four friends of mine---enjoyed the show!

## Gaming

Outer Wilds during the whole week, Donkey Kong during a couple commutes, and a pretty crazy weekend of LAN gaming!

### Ongoing

- **Outer Wilds: Echoes of the Eye** - This DLC is as good as the base game if not better. There's seriously nothing like this out there. I've loved uncovering the new secrets and the mystery behind this paralogue and companion piece to the main story. Beautiful and terrifying stuff. I am not sharing any more spoilers. A huge shout-out to the sound design of this title. The new music is fantastic, yes but the way it is *used* and how it *changes* is fantastic, and the environmental sounds and how they react to the gameplay? Incredible, magnificent work.

- **Donkey Kong '94** - When I got sleepy reading *The House on the Borderland* I decided to put my brain to work finally returning to this little gem and solving the puzzles in a few more levels of the Iceberg world. Having a lot of fun! This game is truly fun and the right amount of challenge too.

### Started

- **Meccha Chameleon** - This is a very silly, and incredibly hilarious "friendslop" game where you control a little stickman that is actually a blank canvas. The point of the game is that you have to hide and paint yourself to fit around in the environment. One of the players turns into a Hunter, and has to search for the others in the world. It's nerver-wracking to see, as the Hunter goes passes by. You get points depending on how bold your hiding spot is as well. Playing as the Hunter and being teased by everyone has some charm too, but hiding is definitely the funniest bit.

- **Astral Ascent** - I tried this game for a whooping 15 minutes, looking at the awesome intro and playing for a bit too. Unfortunately my friends wanted to play something else and I didn't get to complete even the tutorial. Looks awesome and the performance looks great on Switch!

## Reading

Cyberpunk manga, cosmic horror, and of course, soccer.

### Completed

- **Battle Angel Alita** - I finally completed the classic cyberpunk Manga! Some weird moments and crazy sequences, but it was very entertaining. Apparently this had a very abrupt and ugly ending due to the author's health, and then *Last Order* came along to work as a canon finale. However, an original (still kinda rushed) ending was published after *Last Order* which enjoyed even if it's not canon. It definitely got me curious on seeing how the sequel continues. I started this series years ago, so I'm glad to finally see its closure.

- **The House on the Borderland** - That last third of the book really got super trippy. Unfortunately it may have been a little too much compared to the beginning and middle. The ending started to pick up again but then it was over. It was definitely an interesting book, entertaining and very strange especially considering it's a predecessor to Lovecraft and cosmic horror in general. Glad I gave it a shot!

### Ongoing

- **Blue Lock** - Chapter 363-364. The match vs England continues to be quite thrilling. The Blue Lock team definitely has to step up their game as all odds are against them at this point. I am really looking forward to this battle of wits between the managers too.

### Started

- **Battle Angel Alita: Last Order** - Chapter 1-4: The non-canon finale is like three chapters, but this seems like it will take a while!  There's some backstory of the original series, and a whole new bunch of arcs and things to go through for Alita herself. The artstyle has changed but it still feels familiar. Interested to see how this evolves, I'm enjoying the journey.
