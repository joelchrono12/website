## Watching

**Klaus** is an incredible work on animation that works as a retelling of the origin of Santa Claus! It features a mailman who meets an old toy maker, who help bring a very toxic and divided city together for the first time, with the power of acts of kindness! It was a genuinely lovely film with excellent art design and story. A bit weird for October, but it doesn't matter.

## Reading

**The Left Hand of Darkness**. Chapters 6-7. I got a bit slow on this one as I decided to focus on my gaming and was carried away by some manga too! Still, the chapters were also very long and focused on some pretty interesting themes this time around.

**Alita Battle Angel**. Chapters 45-46. After probably more than a year without reading this classic manga of the cyberpunk genre, I finally decided to return to this, with only ten or so chapters, it took me a while to get back into the gist of it. I am having fun nonetheless!

**Blue Lock**. Chapters 360-361. I remember saying I'd stop reading this until the England match was over, but I decided to read some more anyway and ended up getting to a perspective switch outside the match, welp.

**Centuria**. Chapter 80. Not a lot of reading this one this time around! Just some intersting villain backstory stuff.


## Gaming

### Completed

**Final Fantasy IV** has been ongoing for me, for about four years, I have finally completed one of the quintessential entires of the JRPG genre! One of the first with an actual plotline, accompanied by a wonderful soundtrack and turn based combat implementing the ATB system. The PSP version was a joy to go through, I played around 14 hours total since I last touched it last year, just doing endgame dungeons, unlocking ultimate weapons, grinding for levels, and finally beating the last boss, to reach credits and end this story once and for all!

### Ongoing

**Nintendo Switch Sports** was played during the weekent. It was an excellent source of multiplayer fun that I tried with multiple people this week. Both 2-player and 4-player modes! The favorite was volleyball and tennis, as usual.

**Guncho** is a roguelike that I think I've mentioned before. It's a game where you deal with many enemies in a hex grid, with only six bullets and the environment to help you. You can unlock abilities  to swap positions, position yourself, or ricochet bullets, and it's awesome!
