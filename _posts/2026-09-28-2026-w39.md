## Reading

- **The Left Hand of Darkness** - Chapters 1-5. One of the most known Sci-Fi works by Ursula K. Le Guin, this book was chosen for the TWG's Low Key Book Club. Unfortunately, I realized too late and may be unable to complete it for the conversation about it. I'll still read it though. The story is about a human embassador sent to a planet where genders constantly change with the time and seasons, so he is considered a perversion among them. So far, very confusing!

- **Centuria** - Chapters 73-79. Just a few more action packed chapters. I have to say I'm starting to notice why some reviews say this has almost no substance. I can barely remember anything about it, there are no real epic or memorable moments. Only some entertaining twists and such. Revelations are nice, but no pay offs just yet, a bunch of unsolved mysteries and we are 80 chapters in. Hope something happens soon.

## Watching

- **The Day The Earth Stood Still** - A timeless classic from 1951. The writing is smart and holds up well. They somehow avoid mentioning conflicts so you can easily imagine the events happening today, though the Cold War was clearly present. The plot is simple, but ahead of its time. The terror is mostly implied rather than shown. An alien with technology beyond our understanding, along and unstoppable robot companion visit Earth, looking to share a critical message to every leader on the planet: To chill for once and stop the violence around us, *or be turned into dust*.

- **Avengers Endgame Encore** - This was a very, very fun time. I hadn't really watched Endgame since 2019, and it was honestly very interesting to see all of what happened between then and now. Anyway, the movie itself isn't better than *Infinity War*, but I still had plenty of nostalgia and love for these characters, and I had a blast revisiting the closure of the Infinity Saga. Not going to lie, the extra scenes aren't worth much.

- **Minority Report** - A sci-fi film about a pre-crime detective who ends up framed for a pre-crime himself. He has to do everything to prove he is innocent (and will remain so). I only started the film, but I want to see the rest of it (will probably restart lol).
