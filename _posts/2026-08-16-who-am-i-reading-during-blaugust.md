There is a huge list of people participating for Blaugust and you can go to [Andreas' website](https://82mhz.net/posts/2026/08/an-opml-list-of-most-blogs-participating-in-blaugust/) if you want a full OPML file to simply import to your RSS Reader.

Personally, I haven't added too many new people to my feed, but there have been a few which I'll share!

- [Fyr.io](https://fyr.io/) - Matt seems like a pretty cool guy! he is not super active or posting daily, but he's definitely posting plenty of interesting things. The one post that caught my eye this month is the one about [patient gaming](https://fyr.io/post/patient-gaming).

- [Kelson Vibber](https://kvibber.com/) - This guy with a cool name has plenty of thoughts on tech and life, similar to mine. His post on [planned obsolescence](https://journal.kvibber.com/2026/08/planned-obsolescence/) vibed with me. He also reads lots of sci-fi, many works I don't even know of too!

- [WC Robinson](https://wcrobinson.org/) - This is a pretty big website with lots of posts and when I saw their series about the [games that shaped them](https://wcrobinson.org/2026/08/14/blaugust-2026-week-2-the-games-that-shaped-me/) I guess I just had to add them to my RSS feed. Good taste!

Admittedly, I am not following a lot of new people here, and I haven't even reached out to all of these yet. I hope they see this post though and I'll probably send it to them too.

Of course, I am also keeping an eye on some of the friends I have already. Such as:

- [Syl](https://syls.blog), who continues to share her awesome thoughts on gaming and other shenanigans like music and gaming, as well as life updates I enjoy to read.

- [OrbitalMartian](https://orbitalmartian.polymaths.page/) is taking a slower approach, but has plenty of variety regarding tech, software and random life shenanigans.

- [Ariadne](https://noisydeadlines.net/), who keeps sharing her notes on productiviy and tech, plus plenty of music and book recommendations, who end up in my backlog.

- [Sam](https://thetangent.space/) from *The Tangent Space* is a favorite of mine, sharing his history with gaming, weekly digest updates and the like, very good stuff!

- [Jedda](https://notes.jeddacp.com/), despite the difficult moments on her life, has taken this event as a personal challenge, and she is doing fantastically.

- [Naty](https://burgeonlab.com/), a great friend who is passionate about a lot of things and documenting her journey learning about blogging and the indieweb!

There's plenty others I follow, but I'm running out of time today. So here's another short list with a few more!

- [Alex Wolfe](https://wrywriter.ca/)
- [Dave Henry](https://davehenry.blog/)

Enough already, go read those if you don't have enough with my own posts ;)
