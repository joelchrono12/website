I saw Wouter going mad for stickers to put on his laptop and I decided I would do the same!

I have had like a couple dozen loose stickers that I got from AliExpress ages ago, some were gifts and some were included in videogames I've gotten, so I put a mix of eerything here and well, I'm pretty happy with how it turned out!

Behold my glorious creation!

<figure>
<img src="/assets/img/blogs/2026-10-16-laptop-stickers.webp">
<figcaption>My laptop with a bunch of stickers on it, enjoy!</figcaption>
</figure>

The ones I got from AliExpress are the following: The IBM computer, PSP, Game Boy Color, NES Controller, Xbox-style Controller

Some were gifts from friends or family! Such is the case for Kiki---from *Kiki's Delivery Service*---saying "I'll be waiting!", Princess Mononoke---from... *Princess Mononoke*---and Link---from *The Legend of Zelda*---weilding his sword.

There are some that I got for free from StickerMule's  Unix Stickers pack, which I got as a sample: an Intel-like *"Linux Inside"*, the Python, Arch Linux and Debian Linux logos, and of course, Tux!

Two of them came from the *UFO 50* Collectors Edition: A Pig Magician holding a card, and a Pig Knight wearing armor and a sword.

There are also two that were given to me by the AntennaPod team: Their mascot--a microphone with an AntennaPod shirt---and their app logo!

The stickering of my laptop is still ongoing. I would love to get stickers for some projects and logos I've made myself---such as the one for [Clew](https://clew.se) or [Bookrastinating](https://bookrastinating.com)---and for other videogames like *Outer Wilds*, *Metroid* and of course, *Chrono Trigger*, or hobbies such as Speeding and Fountain Pens.

The shipping from Redbubble to my country is rather expensive, so I'm looking for local alternatives to see how much it would cost me, or maybe I'll venture with a huge sticker pack from AliExpress again.

Anyway, I've decided to rank my stickers on a tier list, in plain text because I can't be bothered to make a graphic for that.

```
S: Linux Inside, PSP, Arch, AntennaPod logo, Kiki, Pig Magician,
A: Tux, AntennaPod mascot, Game Boy, Princess Mononoke, Pig Knight
B: IBM PC, NES controller, Link
D: Debian, Python, Xbox Controller
```

I may eventually replace all of the D tier because I just don't feel like they represent me as well as the other stuff.

In any case, I have written way too much about laptop stickers now. Have a good day!
